# Code of Conduct

Velora is a small project run by a handful of maintainers. This page keeps the
small group of people who work on it — maintainers, contributors, and
everyone around the issues, PRs, and release discussions — safe and enjoyable
to be around.

## The short version

Be kind, be patient, and assume good faith. If someone asks you to stop, stop.

## Expected behavior

- Treat everyone with respect, regardless of experience, background, or views.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on the work: make Velora better, not on winning arguments.
- Respect that the maintainers set the direction and have the final say.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, trolling, insults, or personal attacks.
- Doxxing, or sharing anyone's private information without permission.
- Sexually charged language or advances of any kind.
- Other conduct a reasonable person would find unprofessional.

## Enforcement

Anyone can ask someone to stop. If behavior continues, report it directly to
a maintainer. Reports are handled privately and fairly. Depending on severity,
a person may be asked to change, temporarily blocked, or removed from the
project.
